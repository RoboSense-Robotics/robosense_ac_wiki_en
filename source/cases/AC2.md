# AC2

## Delivery Robot

This section demonstrates the autonomous manipulation capability of an embodied robot in a real-world scenario.
In the following video, without any human intervention, the robot smoothly performs a complete workflow that includes picking up items, packaging gifts, placing them on shelves, stable transportation, unpacking boxes, delivering gifts, and even folding and recycling paper boxes. The entire process involves nearly 20 complex long-horizon task steps.

Executing such a series of challenging hand–eye coordinated operations relies heavily on powerful software and hardware support. One of the most prominent hardware highlights is that the robot is equipped with AC2, a high-performance sensor designed specifically for precise robotic manipulation, mounted on both the head and wrist of the robot.

AC2 provides real-time fused data combining short-range, high-precision RGB images and 3D LiDAR point clouds. This hardware-level fusion effectively overcomes the limitations of traditional vision-only solutions in complex lighting conditions and occluded environments.

During the model training phase, the image–point cloud fusion capability of AC2 provides rich 3D spatial structural information, enabling the robot to understand the physical world more comprehensively. This significantly reduces the reliance of the VTLA-3D large model on massive amounts of manually collected data and demonstrates high data efficiency during learning.

During the actual execution (inference) phase of the demo, the high-precision colored point clouds generated by AC2 are deeply fused in the VTLA-3D model together with other multimodal inputs such as force sensing and language instructions. This allows the robot to accurately estimate the spatial pose, material properties, and flexible deformation of target objects (for example, handling soft packaging boxes). Combined with the task planning system, the robot can make rapid real-time decisions, significantly improving the success rate of dexterous long-horizon manipulation tasks.

This case verifies the critical role of AC2 in supporting embodied large-model training and online inference, demonstrating the significant value of AC2 as a multimodal 3D perception sensor in enhancing embodied intelligence systems.
